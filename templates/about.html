ABOUT WILDTRACK

Conservation data,
made easier to explore.

WildTrack is an educational tracker that brings species status, habitat, threats, and conservation actions into one approachable workspace.

01

Explore responsibly

Species profiles are designed for learning and awareness. Representative map points are intentionally broad and should never be treated as exact wildlife locations.

02

Verify current status

Conservation assessments change. Each profile links to the IUCN Red List search so users can verify the most current scientific assessment.

03

Protect sensitive data

The field journal asks only for a general place name. Do not publish nest sites, dens, precise coordinates, or information that could put wildlife at risk.

DATA MODEL

Where the information comes from

The app first tries to load records from a configured Google Sheet. When no Sheet is connected, it safely falls back to the included CSV sample.

  • IUCN links for current category verification
  • Wikipedia background links and profile imagery
  • Representative coordinates for map visualization
  • Local SQLite accounts, watchlists, and field notes
